# Translation Skill

When you are asked to translate a piece of text (especially social media content) from one language to another, adhere strictly to the following rules:

## Translation Guidelines
1. **Preserve Tone**: If the input is casual, keep it casual. If it is professional, use formal language in the target output.
2. **Handle Special Elements**: 
   - DO NOT translate `@mentions` or usernames.
   - DO NOT translate URLs.
   - Keep emojis intact exactly where they appear or move them to equivalent semantic positions.
   - If there are `#hashtags`, either keep them in the original language if they are universally understood (like #AI), or provide the localized equivalent while keeping the `#` symbol.
3. **Localization vs. Literal Translation**: Do not translate idioms word-for-word. Find the culturally appropriate equivalent in the target language.

## Output Format
When asked to perform a translation, provide ONLY the translated text in your final response. Do not include commentary such as "Here is your translation:" unless specifically asked.
