# Hashtag Analysis Skill

When you are asked to analyze or provide hashtags for social media content, follow these rules based on the target platform:

## Platform Strategies

### X (Twitter)
- **Strategy**: Keep it concise. Focus on broad, trending topics.
- **Max limit**: Usually no more than 3-5 tags.
- **Example Tags**: #AI, #Tech, #Innovation, #Startup, #Future, #OpenSource, #MachineLearning

### LinkedIn
- **Strategy**: Professional, career-oriented, and business-focused.
- **Max limit**: Usually 3-6 tags.
- **Example Tags**: #Leadership, #Innovation, #CareerGrowth, #Technology, #Business, #FutureOfWork

### Xiaohongshu (小红书)
- **Strategy**: Lifestyle, discovery-oriented, and highly specific to the niche. Use colloquial tags.
- **Max limit**: Can use up to 10 tags for discoverability.
- **Example Tags**: #好物推荐, #生活方式, #职场干货, #科技前沿, #创业分享, #AI工具, #效率提升

## Instructions
1. Analyze the core topic or entity of the user's content.
2. Select the platform from the list above.
3. Generate a tailored list of hashtags (mixing broad and specific tags) based on the content.
4. Return the hashtags cleanly. If writing a post, append them naturally to the end.
